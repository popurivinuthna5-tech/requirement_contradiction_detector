SECTION 1: BUSINESS GOALS & OPERATIONAL POLICIES
1.1 The business seeks to provide a frictionless multi-channel retail experience for domestic and international shoppers.
1.2 Customer trust, compliance with consumer protection standards, and operational resilience are paramount business objectives.

SECTION 2: CUSTOMER ACCOUNT GOVERNANCE & ACCESS
2.1 Users can change their profile information including phone number and shipping addresses at any time.
2.2 Password recovery must only be performed through administrator verification to prevent unauthorized account takeovers.
2.3 Users must remain logged in for at least 30 minutes without activity to avoid disrupting continuous shopping sessions.
2.4 Administrators must authenticate using two-factor authentication before gaining access to administrative controls.

SECTION 3: SALES & POST-PURCHASE FULFILLMENT
3.1 Orders may be cancelled by customers until dispatch through the online portal.
3.2 Customer refunds shall be processed within 30 days of purchase upon customer return request.
3.3 Dispatch tracking notices shall be automatically generated for all carrier parcels.

SECTION 4: OPERATIONAL METRICS & SUPERVISION
4.1 All web services must provide good performance and reasonable response times under peak load.
4.2 Store managers must be able to export quarterly inventory analytics for auditing.
4.3 Customer support staff shall record resolutions for every customer inquiry.
