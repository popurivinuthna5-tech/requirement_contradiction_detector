SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE
1.1 The E-Commerce Web Platform enables registered consumers to browse catalogs, manage wishlists, place online orders, and configure personal profile information.
1.2 All authenticated interactions must comply with standard security and encryption protocols.

SECTION 2: USER PROFILE & IDENTITY MANAGEMENT
2.1 Users can update their registered email address from their account settings.
2.2 Users must be able to reset their password using their registered email.
2.3 Users must be automatically logged out after 15 minutes of inactivity to protect sensitive financial sessions.
2.4 Two-factor authentication (2FA) via SMS or authenticator app must be strictly mandatory for administrator access.

SECTION 3: ORDER PROCESSING & FULFILLMENT
3.1 Customer can cancel an order before shipment directly through the customer order history dashboard.
3.2 The system shall issue a full refund within 14 days of delivery upon receipt and inspection of returned merchandise.
3.3 Customers may view order tracking milestones in real time.

SECTION 4: SYSTEM PERFORMANCE & ADMINISTRATION
4.1 The system must respond quickly during high traffic periods to ensure a seamless checkout experience.
4.2 Administrators must have access to real-time sales reporting and inventory audit logs.
4.3 Customer support representatives may view customer transaction history for troubleshooting purposes.
